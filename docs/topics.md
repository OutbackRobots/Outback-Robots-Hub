## What is topics.md and how do you use it?

It’s an optional “Browse by topic” landing page.

## Why it exists

Search is great when teachers know what they want. Topics helps when they think:

“I need something on digital safety”

“Show me activities about bias & fairness”

“What lessons cover LLMs?”

## How you use it (simple version)

You manually curate a page with a list of topic tags and links, like:

Digital safety → links to Lesson 4 + relevant activities + glossary items

Data & sensing → links to Lesson 2 + activities + “Data” glossary

This is very low effort and works immediately.

## How you use it (later, nicer version)

Material has a tags feature. With tags enabled, you can:

Tag pages in front matter (tags: [digital-safety, llms])

Have MkDocs automatically generate tag indexes (or we make a nicer Topics page that links into those tag pages)

So for now: topics.md = your curated entry point. Later: it can become partly automated.

If you truly don’t want it yet, we can skip it too — but it’s often helpful early.
